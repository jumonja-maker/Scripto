# Dependency license texts

The bundled PyPI wheels `PySide6_Essentials==6.10.3` and `shiboken6==6.10.3` declare:

`LGPL-3.0-only OR GPL-2.0-only OR GPL-3.0-only`

These unmodified standard license texts are copied from the official Qt for Python repository at tag `v6.10.3`:

- `LGPL-3.0-only.txt`: https://github.com/qtproject/pyside-pyside-setup/blob/v6.10.3/LICENSES/LGPL-3.0-only.txt
- `GPL-3.0-only.txt`: https://github.com/qtproject/pyside-pyside-setup/blob/v6.10.3/LICENSES/GPL-3.0-only.txt
- `GPL-2.0-only.txt`: https://github.com/qtproject/pyside-pyside-setup/blob/v6.10.3/LICENSES/GPL-2.0-only.txt

The LGPL v3 incorporates the GPL v3 text. Inclusion of all alternative license texts does not change the wheel's declared licensing alternatives.

The build preserves all wheel contents, including the original `.dist-info/METADATA`, `RECORD`, and supplied `licenses/LicenseRef-Qt-Commercial.txt`. The downloaded PyPI wheels contain no separate third-party notice files; this does not imply that Qt has no third-party components. Qt source/distribution attribution details must be retained when changing the bundled Qt build. See the project's `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` for dependency source and rebuild links.

Python's embedded distribution retains its original `runtime/LICENSE.txt`, including bundled third-party notices. These license texts cover the relevant third-party components, not ScriptoLib's own application code.
